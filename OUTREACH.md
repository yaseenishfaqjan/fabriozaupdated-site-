# OUTREACH.md — getting FABRIOZA cited by other people

**Why this file exists.** On 24 Aug 2026 a live AI search engine was asked to recommend
custom sportswear manufacturers with low MOQ. It named FABRIOZA — but only because it had
read *our own* blog post. A search for `"FABRIOZA" clothing manufacturer Sialkot` returned
**no third-party mentions at all**: no directory listing, no roundup, no review.

Two rival Sialkot factories, **Ready One** and **Novex Apparel**, appear on other people's
sites — manufacturer.clothing, accio.com, textileinfomedia.com. That is the citation type
AI models weight most heavily, because it isn't self-published.

Everything on the website is now done. **This list is the remaining gap, and it can only be
closed by a human sending messages.** Claude does not send outreach email on your behalf.

## What NOT to do

- No paid link placements or sponsored "awards".
- No fake reviews, fake case studies, or invented client names — this is a standing rule
  on this project.
- **No certification claims except amfori BSCI.** ISO 9001 was removed sitewide on
  9 Sep 2026 after clients (ZYEN, Owain) challenged it - it is not documented. Do not
  claim ISO 9001, OEKO-TEX, WRAP, GOTS, GRS or BCI unless a certificate with a
  verifiable number exists. If amfori BSCI is also undocumented, say so and it goes too.
- No mass-posting the same comment on Reddit or forums; participate genuinely or not at all.
