A university fields a dozen sports and a club runs on a committee that changes every year. Both need the same things from a kit supplier: a minimum that matches one squad rather than a warehouse, names and numbers that do not cost extra, and a price a treasurer can put in front of a committee. That is what this page sets out.

1. The minimum is one squad, not one container

20 pieces per design — enough for a roster, the coaching staff and a few spares. Full kits (jersey, shorts and socks) start at 15 sets.

Most factories that will talk to a university want 300 to 500 pieces per design. That forces you to either over-order for a squad of 22 or pay retail through a middleman. Ours is set where a real team actually sits.

Youth and adult sizes mix freely in the same order. A club running an under-14s side alongside a first team orders once, not twice.

2. Names and numbers cost nothing

Individual player names and numbers are included at no extra charge on sublimation orders. Not a per-name surcharge, not a setup fee, not a minimum of twenty identical shirts before personalisation unlocks.

Send the roster as a spreadsheet with name, number and size per player and we print it as supplied. Add a few blank-backed spares for late signings and we will quote those in the same run.

Design it yourself first. The Team Uniform Designer lets you set your colours, drop your crest on the chest and paste in the squad list, then send the whole thing to us. Names, numbers and sizes arrive with the design, so the first reply is a quotation rather than a list of questions.

3. Try the sizing before you commit the budget

We supply a complete size run sample, XS to 3XL, so the squad can try the actual garment before bulk production starts. Nothing is more expensive than discovering after delivery that the cut runs small across 40 players.

Sizing is the single most common reason a team kit order goes wrong, and it is entirely avoidable. Use the size run.

An athletics department or a multi-sport club does not want a different vendor per code. We make, in our own facility:

The discount applies per style, so four squads of 25 are priced as four orders of 25 — but a single style ordered across the whole club at 500 pieces reaches 30% off. Club hoodies, training tops and polos are usually where that happens.

So from first enquiry to kit in the changing room is realistically 6 to 9 weeks. Start in the close season rather than three weeks before the first fixture, and build in time for the size run — that is the step people skip when they are late, and it is the one that protects the order.

Express bulk in 2 to 3 weeks is possible where the lines allow it. Ask, rather than assume.

6. Reordering mid-season

We keep your approved sample and your patterns, so a reorder for new players runs against the same reference rather than being re-made from a description.

The honest caveat: small colour variation between dye lots is a property of fabric dyeing, not a fault. A reorder placed months later can sit a shade off the original. If that matters to you — and on a playing kit it usually does — order your spares in the first run, when everything is dyed in one lot. It is the cheapest insurance available on a team order.

8. Delivery, duty and the surprise nobody budgets for

We ship by DHL Express, UPS and FedEx with tracking on every shipment. Ask for a delivered duty paid quotation and we settle duty and customs clearance before the kit arrives, so there is no courier invoice landing on a club secretary three days after delivery. That charge is what turns a well-planned kit budget into an awkward committee meeting.

UK clubs should know that Pakistan sits in the Enhanced Preferences tier of the UK's Developing Countries Trading Scheme, so most of what we make enters the UK at 0% duty. Import VAT is separate and still applies.

9. Questions committees ask

Can we see the kit before the whole club commits?

Yes. Order a size run sample, XS to 3XL, in your colours and with your crest. The squad tries it, the committee sees it, and only then does bulk production start.

Can we put sponsor logos on the kit?

Yes, as many positions as you need, and on sublimated kit additional print positions do not add a per-unit cost the way screen printing does. Send the sponsor artwork as a vector PDF. You confirm you have the sponsor's permission to use their marks.

Can different squads have different designs in one order?

Yes. Each design is counted separately against the 20-piece minimum, but they ship together as one consignment, which keeps the freight cost down.

Who owns the design?

You do. Your crest, your colours and your design remain yours, we keep them confidential, we do not publish customer work, and we sign a non-disclosure agreement on request.

How do we check you are a real factory?

Ask for a live video walk-through of the floor, or visit us in Sialkot. Company registration and tax documents are sent with your first quotation on request, before any payment. See how your order is protected.

Duty paid, courier of your choice, and we carry the risk

Duty paid (DDP) on every product, to every country. We settle the import duty and the customs clearance before your goods arrive, so there is nothing to pay on delivery and no courier invoice afterwards. Prefer to clear it yourself, or already have a broker? Take it duty unpaid instead — both are offered on everything we make, at any quantity.

Your courier. DHL Express when you want it fastest, UPS or FedEx when you have an account or a preferred carrier. Tracked either way, with the number sent the day it leaves.

If it does not arrive, that is ours to fix. We chase the courier claim ourselves, and we do not make you wait for it: a shipment lost in transit is remade and reshipped at our cost, or refunded in full including the shipping.
