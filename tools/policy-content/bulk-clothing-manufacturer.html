2. Test the style at 50 before you commit to 5,000

This is the part most factories at volume cannot offer, and it is worth more than a percentage point on unit price.

Most factories quoting you 5,000 pieces have a 1,000-piece minimum. That means your first physical proof of a style is the bulk order itself. If the fit is wrong, the fabric drapes differently than the swatch suggested, or the print sits badly on a size XS, you find out when 5,000 units arrive.

Our minimum is 50 pieces per style. So you can run a style at 50, put it in front of real customers, and only then place the volume order — with the same factory, the same patterns, the same fabric supplier and the same approved sample as the reference. The approved sample from your trial becomes the standard the bulk run is measured against.

A 20-piece trial order is also available for a new style, with the premium credited back against the bulk order.

5. Who checks the goods

Every order, whether it is 50 pieces or 10,000, goes through the same four documented stages:

  1. Fabric inspection before cutting — composition, weight in GSM, shade against the approved lot.
  2. In-line checks during stitching, so a fault is caught at 200 pieces rather than at 5,000.
  3. Finished-garment measurement audit against your tech pack, to ±1 cm (±0.4 in) on body points and ±0.5 cm (±0.2 in) on small points.
  4. AQL 2.5 pre-shipment inspection with photo documentation, which comes to you before the balance is due.

You are welcome to send your own inspector. SGS, Intertek, QIMA or someone you appoint yourself, at fabric stage, in-line, or pre-shipment. You choose them, you pay them, you get the report, and we give them access to the floor. On a first volume order with a supplier you have not met, that is a reasonable thing to want and it has never been a problem here.

You are equally welcome to visit the factory, or to ask for a live video walk-through of your order on the line.

7. Shipping a volume order

We ship worldwide by DHL Express, UPS and FedEx, with tracking on every shipment and the tracking number sent on the day of dispatch. Transit is 3 to 8 business days depending on destination.

Duty is handled one of two ways, stated on your quotation: paid by the receiver on arrival, or delivered duty paid, where we settle duty and customs clearance and nothing is owed on delivery. UK buyers should read why UK duty is 0% on most of what we make — Pakistan sits in the Enhanced Preferences tier of the UK's Developing Countries Trading Scheme, which a supplier in China does not.

8. What we need to quote you accurately

Send these and the first reply is a real quotation rather than a list of questions:

  • The garment, and a reference picture or an existing sample you can post to us.
  • Quantity per style, and the split across sizes and colours.
  • Fabric: composition and weight in GSM, or tell us the handfeel you want and we will propose options.
  • Decoration: screen print, DTF, sublimation, embroidery, and how many positions.
  • Labels, hang tags and packaging requirements.
  • Destination country, for the freight and duty estimate.
  • The date you need goods in hand.

Artwork is best sent as a vector PDF with fonts outlined. A tech pack is welcome but not required — if you do not have one, we can work from measurements and a reference garment, and our tech pack template is free to use.

9. Questions volume buyers ask

Can you hold stock and ship in drops?

Yes. We can produce the full quantity and release it against a schedule you set, which keeps your working capital and your warehouse free. Storage beyond 30 days is quoted separately.

Can you run several styles in one order?

Yes, and the volume discount applies per style rather than across the order, because the cost driver is the setup for each style: pattern, screens, digitizing and fabric. Four styles at 1,000 pieces each are priced at the 1,000-piece rate, not the 4,000-piece rate.

Will a repeat order match the first one?

We keep your approved sample and your patterns, so a repeat runs against the same reference. The honest caveat is dye lots: small colour variation between runs is a property of fabric dyeing, not a fault. Where exact consistency matters across a repeat, tell us at quotation stage and the whole quantity can be dyed in one lot.

Do you work with existing brands and retailers?

Yes, and we do not publish who. Since 13 October 2026 our standing rule is that no customer work appears on this website at all, including in a portfolio. We sign a non-disclosure agreement on request, before you send artwork if you prefer.
