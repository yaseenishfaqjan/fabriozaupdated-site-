FABRIOZA is a clothing manufacturer, not a data business. We collect what an order needs and nothing more, we never sell it, and we never see your full card number. This page sets out exactly what we hold, why, who else touches it, and how to have it deleted.

1. Who is responsible for your data

FABRIOZA, a sole proprietorship registered in Pakistan, owned by Muhammad Yasin Ishfaq, National Tax Number 3130171989577, of Saro Street, near Fateh Garh Road, Sialkot 51310, Punjab, Pakistan. For any question about this policy, email info@fabrioza.com.

2. What we collect

When you enquire or order

  • Your name and the name of your company or brand.
  • Email address, and telephone or WhatsApp number.
  • Delivery address, and the consignee details a courier and customs require.
  • Artwork, logos, size charts, tech packs and any other order files you send us.
  • The content of your enquiry and our correspondence about your order.
  • Invoice and payment records, including which method was used and when it cleared.

When you visit the website

Pages viewed, approximate location at city level, referring site, device and browser type, and how you move through a page. This is collected by the analytics tools named in section 6.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for and do not hold your full card number, card expiry date or security code. A card payment is taken on Safepay's own secure page, and the card details are entered there rather than given to us. We receive confirmation that a payment succeeded, the amount, and the last four digits for reconciliation.

3. Why we use it

  • To prepare a quotation and answer your questions.
  • To make, inspect, pack and ship your order, and to produce the photographs and video you approve before dispatch.
  • To arrange shipping and complete customs documentation.
  • To take payment and to issue invoices and receipts.
  • To keep accounting and tax records.
  • To contact you about your order, and about a repeat or follow-on order.

The basis for all of it is the performance of the contract you are entering into with us, and our legitimate interest in running and accounting for a manufacturing business. We do not use your order data for advertising.

Your data leaves Pakistan when it reaches a courier, a payment processor or a hosting provider located elsewhere. That transfer happens only to the extent the service requires.

5. Cookies

The website uses cookies and similar browser storage for two purposes: to keep the site working, including a security token that protects the enquiry form from abuse, and to collect the analytics in section 6.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The enquiry form and the site itself will still work; the analytics will not record your visit.

6. Analytics, and what they record

We use two third-party tools on this website, and it is worth being specific about what the second one does:

  • Google Analytics 4 — aggregate traffic measurement: how many people arrive, from where, and which pages they read.
  • Microsoft Clarity — this records how a page is used, including mouse movement, clicks, scrolling and the sequence of pages in a visit, and produces a replay of that session and heatmaps. It is used to find pages that confuse people. Clarity masks text entered into form fields.

Both operate under their own privacy policies and their own retention settings, which we do not control. You can prevent both by blocking cookies for this site, or by using your browser's or operating system's tracking protection.

We do not run advertising pixels on this website.

7. How long we keep it

  • Order, invoice and payment records: six years after the order, which is the record-keeping period our accountant works to for Pakistani tax.
  • Artwork and production files: for as long as you are an active customer, so a repeat order can be made to the same specification, and deleted on request at any time.
  • Enquiries that do not become orders: up to two years, then deleted.
  • Website analytics: held by Google and Microsoft under their own retention periods.

8. How it is kept secure

The website is served over HTTPS throughout. Enquiry submissions are protected by a server-side security token. Order records and correspondence are held in accounts restricted to FABRIOZA staff who need them, each with its own login.

No system is beyond compromise, and we will not claim otherwise. If a breach ever affected your data we would tell you, by email, with what we know and what we are doing about it.

9. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • tell you what we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your data, including your artwork and production files;
  • stop sending you anything that is not about an order in progress.

Email info@fabrioza.com. We reply within one working day and complete the request within 30 days. There is no charge.

Deletion has one limit worth stating honestly: we cannot delete an invoice we are required to keep for tax. Everything else goes.
