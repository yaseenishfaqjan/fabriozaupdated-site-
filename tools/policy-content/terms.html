5. What we need from you

Delay on an order is more often caused by missing information than by the factory, so these matter:

  • Artwork as a vector PDF, with fonts embedded or outlined. Raster files enlarge badly and we will tell you rather than print something that will disappoint you.
  • Measurements, as a size chart with a tolerance, or an existing garment sent to us to copy.
  • Colours, as Pantone references where colour accuracy matters.
  • Confirmation that you hold the rights to every logo, name, image and design you send us. By sending artwork you confirm you own it or are licensed to use it, and that it does not infringe anyone else's trade mark, copyright or design right.

8. Tolerances

Garments are made by people from woven and knitted cloth, and both move. These are the tolerances every order is judged against, measured against your approved sample:

MeasuredTolerance
Body measurements, such as chest, length and sleeve±1 cm (±0.4 in)
Small points, such as cuff, collar and pocket±0.5 cm (±0.2 in)
Fabric weight in GSM±5%

Small colour variation between dye lots is normal and is not a defect. Two production runs of the same colour will not match exactly. Where exact consistency across a repeat order matters, tell us at quotation stage so the whole quantity can be dyed in one lot.

Goods within these tolerances and matching the approved sample are correct. Goods outside them are covered by section 4 of the Refund and Return Policy.
