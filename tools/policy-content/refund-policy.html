Every garment FABRIOZA makes is cut and sewn to one customer's approved specification. Nothing we ship exists before you order it, and nothing goes into production until you have approved a physical sample. That shapes this policy, so it is worth stating the consequence plainly at the top: we cannot accept a return because an order is no longer wanted. What we do accept, and pay for, is our own mistake.

1. Custom production and change of mind

We hold no ready-made stock. Fabric is bought for your order, labels are woven with your brand, screens and embroidery files are made for your artwork, and the garments are cut to your measurements. None of it can be resold to anyone else.

For that reason a return for change of mind is not accepted once production has started. This is the normal position for cut-and-sew manufacturing and it is why the approval stages below exist: they are there so that you decide before money is committed, not after.

2. You approve the order at four stages

Nothing proceeds to the next stage without your written approval, by email or WhatsApp.

StageWhat you approve
FabricComposition, weight in GSM, colour and handfeel
Artwork or mockupPrint and embroidery placement, size and colours
SampleThe physical garment, made in your fabric to your measurements
Before dispatchPhotographs and video of the finished goods

The approved sample is the standard the bulk order is judged against. We keep an identical one. If a dispute ever arises about whether the goods are correct, it is settled by comparing the delivered garments with that sample rather than by argument.

3. Cancellation and deposits

Before production or material purchase has started

Your deposit is refunded, less any cost already incurred on your behalf. Those costs are real and are itemised for you rather than estimated. They typically include embroidery digitizing, screen preparation, woven labels and hang tags already produced with your brand, and fabric bought specifically for your order.

After cutting or production has started

Deposits are not refundable. Once fabric is cut to your pattern it has no other use, and the labour booked against your order cannot be recovered.

Sample fees

Sample fees are not refundable once sampling has started. The fee pays for the pattern, the fabric and the machine time that produce a garment made only for you.

4. Manufacturing defects and measurement tolerance

If goods arrive with a manufacturing defect, or outside the agreed measurement tolerance when compared with the approved sample, we put it right at our own cost.

Report it within 7 days of delivery, to info@fabrioza.com, with photographs and video showing the problem and, where it is a measurement issue, a tape measure against the garment. The 7 days run from the date the courier records delivery.

Where a defect is confirmed, FABRIOZA will at its own cost either remake or repair the affected pieces, including the replacement shipping, or refund those pieces. Which of the three applies is agreed with you; a remake is usually fastest because the fabric and pattern are already in the factory.

The remedy covers the affected pieces. A defect found on part of an order does not void the order as a whole.

5. What this policy does not cover

  • Damage caused by misuse, or by washing against the care instructions on the garment label.
  • Small colour variation within normal dyeing tolerance. Dye lots are not identical, and a slight difference between two production runs of the same colour is a property of fabric dyeing rather than a fault.
  • Any garment that matches the sample you approved. If the approved sample was wrong, the approval stages in section 2 are where that is caught.
  • Courier delay, and customs inspection or clearance delay. Those are covered in our Shipping and Delivery Policy.

The measurement and fabric tolerances that apply to every order are set out in section 7 of our Terms and Conditions: ±1 cm (±0.4 in) on body measurements, ±0.5 cm (±0.2 in) on small points, and ±5% on fabric weight.

6. How a refund is paid

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 7 to 14 business days of approval. A payment made by card is refunded to the same card; we cannot send a card refund to a different card or to a bank account, because the refund travels back along the original authorisation. A payment made by bank transfer is returned to the sending account.

Bank charges levied by an intermediary bank on an international transfer are outside our control and are not part of the refunded amount.

7. If something has gone wrong

Contact us first. Email info@fabrioza.com or message WhatsApp +92 312 4815601, quoting your invoice number. We answer within one working day, Monday to Saturday.

Almost every problem of this kind is a production fault with an obvious remedy, and we would much rather remake a batch than have a customer pursue a dispute. Raising it with us directly is also the fastest route to having the goods you actually ordered.
