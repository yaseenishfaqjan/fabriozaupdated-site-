How it works

  1. Send your requirements or tech pack. Composition, GSM and colour if you know them; a reference garment's details if you do not.
  2. We confirm the fabric specification in writing within 24 hours, so what is being made is agreed before anything is made.
  3. Fabric prepared in 4–6 working days from your approval of that specification.
  4. Photos and video of your fabric for approval. Nothing is cut until you have seen it and said yes.
  5. Sample garment in your own fabric, then bulk production against the approved sample.
  6. Photos and videos before shipment, then delivered door to door by express courier — DHL, UPS or FedEx.

Worth being plain about one thing: approval happens through photographs and video, and then through the physical sample garment. We do not post fabric swatches ahead of an order.

Questions brands ask

What is the minimum order?

300 pieces per order for custom fabric development. That can be split across sizes and colourways within the order.

How long does the fabric take?

4 to 6 working days after you approve the specification. Garment production follows the fabric, and the lead time for that depends on the garment and the decoration.

Can I match a fabric I already have?

Yes. Send the composition, the GSM and photographs, or the details of a reference garment, and we will quote the closest development and tell you honestly where it will differ.

Can you match my Pantone colour?

Yes. The fabric is dyed to your code, and you approve the result by photo and video before anything is cut.

Is this available for samples only?

No. Custom fabric is for bulk orders of 300+ pieces. A sample can be made first in our closest stock fabric so you can judge the pattern and fit before committing to your own cloth.

How do I approve the fabric?

Photographs and video at every stage: the fabric itself, then cutting, decoration and the finished garments before they ship.

What are the payment terms?

50% deposit and 50% before dispatch. On orders of 1,000 pieces and over it is 70% deposit and 30% before dispatch, and a Letter of Credit at sight is accepted from 5,000 pieces. See payment methods.
