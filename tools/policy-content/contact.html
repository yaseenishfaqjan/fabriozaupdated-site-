What to include, so the first reply is a real answer

You will get a useful quotation instead of a list of questions if your first message covers:

  • What you want made — the garment, and a reference picture or link if you have one.
  • Quantity, and the split across sizes and colours if you know it.
  • Fabric, as a composition and weight in GSM if you have a preference, or tell us the feel you are after and we will suggest options.
  • Branding — screen print, DTF, sublimation, embroidery, woven labels, hang tags, and whether you need custom packaging.
  • Your destination country, so the quotation can include an estimated shipping cost.
  • Your deadline, if you have one.

Artwork is best sent as a vector PDF with fonts embedded or outlined. If all you have is a logo in a raster format, send it anyway and we will tell you whether it will reproduce cleanly.

Visiting the factory

You are welcome to visit. We are in Sialkot, about three hours by road from Lahore and Islamabad, and roughly 15 minutes from Sialkot International Airport. Tell us a day or two ahead so someone is free to walk you through production rather than meeting you at a desk.

If flying to Pakistan to check on a first order is not a sensible use of your week, ask for a live video walk-through instead. We do them regularly and will show you whatever you want to see.

Before you place a first order

Three things people reasonably want before sending money to a factory they have never visited, all of which we are happy to provide:

  • Company documents — registration and tax papers, sent with your first quotation on request, before any payment.
  • A sample — a physical garment in your fabric, with your labels, to your measurements. Nothing goes into bulk production until you have approved one.
  • A card invoice — paying by card gives you recourse through your own bank, which a transfer does not. See Payment Methods and how your order is protected.
